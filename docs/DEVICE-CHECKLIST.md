# Quiet iPhone acceptance checks

1. Launch the signed Release build, version 1.1.0. Check that Home lists Instagram and TikTok, and relaunch after onboarding.
2. Toggle iOS light and dark appearance. Check Home, Settings, Instagram setup, the inbox, a conversation, the composer, and the status bar. Try larger system text and Reduce Motion. Scroll quickly through a long conversation and open and dismiss the keyboard.
3. Connect Instagram through Beeper Cloud, then sign in to Quiet with a Beeper Matrix password. Verify the device with a recovery code if prompted. Connect Beeper to a second supported service if available and confirm its conversations stay out of the Instagram inbox.
4. Compare a recent conversation with Beeper. Search for it, open it, scroll up to older messages, and receive a new text while Quiet is open. Send a test text to a consenting recipient and confirm it arrives in the official Instagram app. Repeat after reconnecting to the internet and after relaunch.
5. Disconnect the phone from Windows and test sending and receiving over Wi-Fi or cellular. AltServer is used for Apple signing and refresh.
6. Disconnect the account in Quiet. Verify sign-in is required again and previously loaded messages are cleared.
7. Launch the Instagram shortcut with Quiet open and closed. Confirm a TikTok shortcut lands on its unavailable screen. Try the old Snapchat or YouTube shortcuts and check that neither opens an old integration.
